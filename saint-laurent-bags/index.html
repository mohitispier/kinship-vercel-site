Comparing materials and design details

Leather bags can take many designs. Their look is influenced by several things, such as the texture, grain, finish, and flexibility of the leather used to make them.

Here are the elements I will look at when analysing a product:

  • The type of leather, according to the description
  • The size of the bag
  • Stitching and finish
  • The length of the straps and handles
  • Zipper and hardware
  • Interior pockets and the bag’s closure mechanism

I will also examine the product photographs, because viewing a bag from different angles shows what it really looks like. This matters, since some details may not be mentioned in the description.
